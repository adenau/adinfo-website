Technology has become inseparable from business success, but technical expertise alone is not enough. The real challenge lies in aligning technology decisions with a clear vision, ensuring that teams, tools, and processes all work toward the same goals. That is where ADInfo comes in.

We specialize in supporting IT leadership, helping organizations turn technology into a strategic advantage. This includes guiding executives on how to make the most of emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence, coaching new leaders in best practices, and reshaping workflows for efficiency. Our goal is to help organizations move from reactive problem-solving to proactive innovation.

### Leadership Training for Technology Teams

Strong leadership is the foundation of successful IT departments. We work with managers, team leads, and executives to build the skills required to lead effectively in a fast-changing technology landscape. This now includes understanding how to lead teams through the adoption and responsible use of AI-driven tools.

Our training focuses on:

-   Understanding the role of leadership in technical contexts
-   Communicating effectively between technical and non-technical stakeholders
-   Managing change and guiding teams through transformation
-   Making informed technology decisions, including when and how to adopt AI, with long-term impact in mind

### Roadmap Development and Strategic Planning

A technology roadmap is more than a schedule of upgrades. It is a living strategy that aligns business needs with the right tools, systems, and talent. Increasingly, this means evaluating where AI-powered solutions can deliver competitive advantages, such as improving operational efficiency or enhancing customer experiences.

ADInfo helps organizations define a clear vision and then translate that vision into actionable steps. This includes:

-   Auditing existing systems and services, including AI readiness
-   Identifying priorities for growth, modernization, or consolidation
-   Establishing milestones, budgets, and success metrics
-   Ensuring every technology choice, including AI adoption, supports broader business objectives

### Workflow Auditing and Process Improvement

Technology can only deliver its full potential when processes are efficient, transparent, and well-structured. ADInfo evaluates how work flows through your organization, where delays occur, where communication breaks down, and where automation could bring measurable gains.

Our recommendations often include the strategic integration of artificial intelligence to streamline repetitive tasks, improve decision-making through predictive analytics, and free teams to focus on high-value work. By combining process improvement with emerging tools, we ensure your organization remains competitive and adaptable in a rapidly evolving digital landscape.

### A Partner in Technology Leadership

Alexandre Denault is VP of Production and Technology at Hyper Hippo, where he leads production and central technology teams. His experience spans more than 20 years in technology and software development in the games industry, including systems supporting millions of daily users and projects with more than a decade in production.

His approach brings together hands-on experience in development, system administration, DevOps and LiveOps, Agile coaching, and online infrastructure. He believes that strong engineering culture and thoughtful production practices give creative teams the foundation they need to do their best work.
